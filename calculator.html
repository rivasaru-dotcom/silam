UNDERSTAND YOUR RESULT

A number is useful. A strategy is better.

Your repayment estimate is only one part of the picture. A broker can help you consider rates, loan structure, fees, deposit, lender policies and your broader financial goals.

01

Interest rate

A small change in your interest rate can make a meaningful difference to repayments over the life of a loan.

02

LVR

LVR compares the amount you borrow with the value of the property and can affect available lending options.

03

Loan term

A longer loan term can reduce each repayment but may increase the total interest paid over time.

04

Extra repayments

Paying extra can reduce the balance faster and potentially reduce the interest paid over the life of the loan.
